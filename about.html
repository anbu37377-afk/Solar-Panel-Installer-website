About Us

Our History

Building a Sustainable Future Since 2012

SolarGrid started with a simple mission: to make solar energy accessible to everyone. What began as a small local team has grown into a leading solar installation provider in the region.

We believe in quality, transparency, and the power of the sun to change the world. Our certified engineers work tirelessly to design systems that maximize your savings and minimize your carbon footprint.

Our Mission

To accelerate the world's transition to sustainable energy through affordable and reliable solar solutions.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted solar partner for homeowners and businesses globally, fostering a cleaner planet.
