10 Mobile Tips to Maintain Your Solar Panels

Solar panels are a great investment, but they require some care to ensure they operate at peak efficiency.

Keeping your panels clean is the most important step. Dust, leaves, and bird droppings can reduce output by up to 25%. Here is a comprehensive guide to maintaining your system.

1. Regular Cleaning

Use a soft hose to wash off dust. Avoid high-pressure washers as they can damage the connection points.

2. Trim Nearby Trees

Shadows are the enemy of solar power. Ensure trees don't grow to shade your roof during peak sunlight hours.

"A well-maintained solar system can last up to 30 years with minimal degradation in performance."

For more technical maintenance, always contact a certified professional.
